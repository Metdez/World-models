# 09-02 PLAN — Powertrain, brakes and aids

**Objective**: full `drive/powertrain.ts` (ARCH-M2 §7.7) and new `drive/aids.ts` (§7.8: ABS, stability, the traction
scale curve and the keyboard steering helpers), simple and labelled "Assist: simplified model", each switchable through
`DriveAids`, each reporting through the frozen `frame.drive` flags (`absActive`, `tractionActive`, `stabilityActive`).
Frozen signatures kept so the v0 plant keeps compiling; plant wiring is 09-04's. Brake bias stays at gtCoupe's 0.62.

**Owned files**: `src/sim-core/drive/powertrain.ts`, `src/sim-core/drive/aids.ts`, `src/sim-core/content/gt-coupe-powertrain.ts`,
`tests/sim-core/{powertrain,aids}.test.ts`.

## Tasks
1. **Powertrain** — keep the v0 body, fix: kick-down threshold below the full-throttle upshift rpm (0.92·redline = 6440 >
   upFull 6400 would hunt), multi-gear kick-down and downshift (stopping in 4th must not leave the car in 4th), reverse
   cap tapered, TCS target from `aids.ts`; add `brakeTorques()` (pedal × maxTorque × brakeShare/2, handbrake on the
   rears). Tests (fake io, P1): upshift light vs full throttle, no hunting on slow up/down speed ramps at fixed throttle,
   kick-down, cut/ramp/lockout timing, Jolt LSD split, engine braking at 4000 rpm = a + b·rpm, reverse cap and 0.5
   throttle scale, no creep at zero throttle, reset gear choice, brake split.
2. **Aids** — `createAids` (ABS pressure factors, stability selective braking + throttle scale with a short hold),
   `tractionTarget`, `steeringLimit`, `countersteerHelp`. Tests: ABS keeps κ ≥ −0.2 on a fake wheel with a saturating
   tyre (and the wheel locks with ABS off), TCS curve, oversteer → outer front / understeer → inner rear, mirrored
   left/right, aids off → identity, flags.

**Verification**: `npx vitest run tests/sim-core/powertrain.test.ts tests/sim-core/aids.test.ts`, then `npm run check`
(M1 goldens unchanged, det-math arch test green).
