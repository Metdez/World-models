# 09-04 PLAN — Plant integration, safety and validation

## Objective
Make the driven car honest and comfortable: wire the 09-01 tyre/compliance, 09-02 powertrain/aids and the §7.6 jacking
into the plant in the §7.2 order, finish safety/sleep/countersteer help, sweep car-model/ride7dof onto det kernels
(Auto goldens unchanged), and prove it with the §11 validation matrix plus a per-suspension honesty report.

## Tasks
1. **Det sweep**: car-model Math.sin/cos/tan/atan → dsin/dcos/dtan/datan (09-03: bit-identical to V8), ride7dof
   Math.tanh → dtanh (checked here: 0 mismatches vs Math.tanh on 3.5 M arguments); det-math test covers ALL sim-core;
   goldens unchanged.
2. **Integration** (§7.2 full order): brakeTorques → AidsIo → aids.step (ABS, stability, throttle scale) → powertrain
   with `peakSlipRatio(rear)`; compliance toe/camber per wheel from the previous step's forces (filter τ, clamp
   ±maxAngle; `maxAngle = 0` reproduces the kinematics-only plant bit-for-bit); jacking via optional
   `RideInput.linkVertical` (absent → M1 arithmetic byte-for-byte; symmetric case = M1 pair to 1e-9); HumanDriver uses
   `assistedSteer` (countersteer help); snapshot/restore of aids state, compliance filters and the driver ring; flags
   absActive/stabilityActive. Test-only plant options (linear tyre, rigid loads) for T1/T2.
3. **Safety + ground**: NaN containment counts and restores; sleep on a 5 % slope (< 1 mm / 10 s); check wheel loads
   across the road edge onto grass at several places of the loop (fix `world/**` if the ground is discontinuous).
4. **Validation** (`tests/sim-core/handling-*.test.ts`, `plant-*.test.ts`): T1–T8, T11–T18, T20, S1–S3, H1, N1 with the
   accepted (widened) bands; printed per-suspension report and honesty table (steady state, step steer, ISO 3888-2
   style lane change, brake in turn incl. rear compliance-toe sign, bump strip) and the clumsy-keyboard drivability runs.
