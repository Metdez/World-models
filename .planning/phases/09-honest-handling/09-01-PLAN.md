# 09-01 PLAN — Tyre model and compliance derivation

**Objective**: replace the v0 tyre body with the §7.4 model (unit-slope Magic Formula combined-slip curve, camber
thrust as equivalent slip, falling branch when sliding, deflection clamp moved past the peak) and derive per-axle
compliance coefficients (§7.5) from OUR hardpoints with ONE shared role-based bushing table. Signatures frozen; the
plant (09-04) integrates.

**Owns**: `src/sim-core/drive/tyre.ts`, `src/sim-core/drive/compliance.ts`, `src/sim-core/content/gt-coupe-tyres.ts`,
`tests/sim-core/{tyre,compliance}.test.ts`, `tests/fixtures/09-01-*`.

## Tasks
1. **Tyre body + Y1** — MF curve f(s) = sin(C atan(Bs − E(Bs − atan Bs))), B = 1/C via det kernels; camber
   tanA = uy/σy − camberRatio·inclination; peak slip solved once per axle; `limitDeflection` bound moved to a multiple
   of the peak slip (falling branch kept for a locked/spinning wheel at speed, no wind-up at standstill); relaxation
   helper (backward Euler, the plant's formula). Tests: signs, camber/toe sweeps, Cα(N) and μ(N), friction circle,
   relaxation steady state and lag, continuity through v = 0, low-speed damping bounds.
2. **Compliance derivation + C1** — 6×6 Plücker stiffness at the design contact patch (distance links at the
   chassis-bush rate of their role, strut top mount as two radial wrenches, locked spring at the wheel rate), unit
   loads → fyToe, fyCamber, fxToeBrake, fxToeDrive, mzToe, lateralStiffness. Tests: properties (½ rates → 2×; ×1000 →
   ≈ 0; mirror), gates, printed per-type report with the ±2× bushing band.
