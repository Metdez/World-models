# 04-01 PLAN — Camera rig + attract mode

**Objective:** the `camera` feature poses the renderer's camera (CAMERA service) with three live views
(follow / explore / closeup), smooth frame-rate-independent blends and director shots; the `attract` feature turns
the app into an unattended 60 s auto-directed loop after 30 s idle (or `?attract=1`) and restores the user's state
on any input.

**Owns:** `src/features/camera/**`, `src/features/attract/**`, `tests/features/camera.test.ts`,
`tests/features/attract.test.ts`, `tests/e2e/04-01-camera.spec.ts`.

## Design
- **Car frame** for every pose: origin = driving-line point at the frame's (interpolated) `lapTime` from the pure
  `sim.current.stateAt()`, yaw = path heading. It is smooth by construction (no ride jitter, no lag at speed).
  Poses are authored and blended in this frame, so a blend never lags behind a car doing 40 m/s.
- **follow:** chase offset from a damped heading (`1-exp(-λdt)`, reveals the car's side in corners), distance,
  height and FOV eased with speed, look target pulled toward `stateAt(lapTime + Δ)` (pre-turns into corners),
  camera kept above the road surface.
- **explore:** camera-controls on a proxy camera; its target follows the car (`moveTo(…, false)`) and the orbit
  turns with the car's yaw so a 3/4 view stays 3/4 through corners. Enabled only in explore.
- **closeup:** rigid body-frame anchor per corner (`focusCorner`, `camera:focus`), framed into the wheel well from
  front/rear-outboard, x-ray switched on (and restored on leaving if the user did not touch it). 2-corner aware.
- **Blend:** smootherstep 1.2 s; position interpolated in spherical coordinates about the car centre (arcs around
  the car, never through it), orientation slerp, FOV lerp. Reduced motion: 0.2 s fade + cut instead of motion.
- **Director shots:** chase, orbit, closeup, trackside (tripod ahead on the inside of the line, telephoto FOV),
  overhead, front-view. `clearShot()`, a view change or `user:activity` returns to the view.
- **Attract:** emits `user:activity` from DOM input; idle 30 s → `store.attract` (not in e2e); a pure storyboard
  timeline (60 s, ≤ 8-word captions, no digits) drives store patches, section seeks (by phenomenon, behind a fade)
  and shots; any input exits with a fade and restores the snapshot of every key it touched.
