# 03-01 PLAN — Track view

**Objective:** render the proving loop as a premium, minimal proving ground whose road surface IS the simulation's
surface: every ribbon vertex sits at `roadHeight(s, l)` of the built track, with finer rows/columns where the
closed-form features live, so wheels touch the drawn road and the features are visible where the car reacts.

**Owns:** `src/features/track/**`, `tests/features/track.test.ts`, `tests/e2e/03-01-track.spec.ts`.
**Consumes:** RENDER (scene, quality), `ctx.sim.current.track` (BuiltTrack), `ctx.assets` (CC0 texture sets), store.quality.

## Tasks
1. **Ribbon + materials/markings** (`ribbon.ts`, `materials.ts`)
   - Rows at the tier's `trackResolution`, uniform so the loop closes. Zone rows are added for features:
     bump/pothole 5 cm, kerb strike 10 cm, washboard 7.5 cm, humps 15 cm, pavé at stone centres (high: + joints).
   - Columns: the ISO-rail multiples of 0.8 m across the asphalt (the roughness is exactly piecewise linear there),
     band edges, plus each feature's lateral extent.
   - Consecutive rows with different columns are stitched by a merge walk, so there are no T-junction cracks.
   - Bands become material groups: asphalt, cobbles (asphalt inside the pavé), kerb, apron, grass.
     UV = (l, s) in metres. A duplicated closing row gets averaged normals, so there is no seam at s = L.
   - MeshStandardMaterial + `onBeforeCompile`:
     - AA edge lines, start-line checker, section ticks, yellow feature bars, rubbered wheel tracks;
     - kerb stripes painted at corners and at the kerb strike;
     - world-space grass shared with the terrain.
2. **Terrain, props, quality** (`terrain.ts`, `props.ts`, `index.ts`)
   - Variable-spacing grid out to the fogged horizon. It sits just under the runoff edge, dips away from the
     track and is hidden under the asphalt.
   - Instanced cones from `track.props`; merged section signboards (one canvas atlas, one draw call).
   - Anisotropy follows `store.quality`.
   - Hide the placeholder's ground disc and centreline (temporary shortcut until the orchestrator deletes the
     placeholder).
3. **Tests + e2e + visual review**
   - `tests/features/track.test.ts`: every vertex equals `toWorld`/`roadHeight` at its (s, l); feature zones are
     dense; closing-row match; no cracks (stitch covers the band area); groups cover all indices.
   - `tests/e2e/03-01-track.spec.ts`: boot with track, draw calls ≤ budget, zero console errors and warnings,
     screenshots at several lap times.
   - Scratch harness (not committed) with a free camera for overview shots. Look at the shots and iterate.
