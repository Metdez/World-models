# 03-02 PLAN — Renderer post stack + environment

**Wave** 1 (phase 3) · **Depends** Phase 1 (started early: Phase 2 runs in parallel; nothing here depends on it —
the stub circle sim is enough) · **Parallel with** 03-01, 03-03, 03-04 and phase-2 executors (disjoint files)

## Objective
Premium, restrained look that holds on a real GPU and stays cheap: a pmndrs `postprocessing` composer
(HalfFloat buffers; Render → N8AO half-res (high only) → ONE merged EffectPass: SMAA + Bloom + Vignette + AgX tone
map, dithered) reacting to `store.quality`; the §10 adaptive pixel-ratio governor (pure, unit-tested); an
`environment` feature with the HDRI per view (follow/explore: derelict_airfield, closeup: ferndale_studio), sun
peak clamped before PMREM, blurred background, fog matched to the horizon, one directional sun extracted from the
HDRI that follows the car with a texel-snapped shadow sized from quality, and a contact blob under the car.
`RenderService.compile()` warms scene programs, the shadow pass and the post shaders.

## Owned files
`src/features/renderer/**`, `src/features/environment/**`, `tests/features/renderer-governor.test.ts`,
`tests/features/environment.test.ts` (§3: feature-owner test), `tests/e2e/03-02-env.spec.ts` (+ PLAN, SUMMARY).

## Tasks
1. **Composer + quality + governor.** `renderer/post.ts` (composer, lazily built EffectPass per bloom/smaa key,
   lazy N8AO, resize, quality apply), `renderer/governor.ts` (pure §10 logic), `renderer/n8ao.d.ts` (n8ao ships no
   types). `renderer/index.ts`: `antialias:false, depth:false, stencil:false`, `NoToneMapping`, shadow map on (PCF),
   render through the composer, start tier `(pointer: coarse)` → low, governor off in e2e / with `?quality=`.
   Test: `tests/features/renderer-governor.test.ts`.
2. **Environment.** `environment/hdri.ts` (pure: clamp RGBE→half data in place by bit compare, sun direction /
   colour / removed irradiance, horizon colour), `environment/index.ts` (HDRLoader → clamp → PMREM, per-view swap,
   blurred background, fog, sun + texel snap, contact blob, quality reaction). Placeholder gets a ground disc that
   receives the shadow (deleted with the placeholder). Test: `tests/features/environment.test.ts`.
3. **Compile hook + e2e.** `compile()` = `compileAsync` + one warm composer render (shadow + post programs).
   `tests/e2e/03-02-env.spec.ts`: low and high tiers boot with zero console errors AND warnings, post on at high,
   view swap to closeup, non-blank canvas; DPR-1 screenshots to the scratchpad, inspected by eye.
