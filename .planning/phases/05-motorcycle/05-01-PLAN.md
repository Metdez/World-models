# 05-01 PLAN — Motorcycle simulation + content

**Objective:** `createMotoModel` implements the VehicleModel seam (ARCHITECTURE §7.2.4) for the cafe racer with three
presets whose differences come from geometry only (research 08): planar kinematics LUT, 4-DOF in-plane ride with the
longitudinal coupling `E = S − Fx·dx/dw`, lean/steer tracker. `createSimulation` runs the bike unchanged.

**Owns:** `src/sim-core/moto/**` (model.ts planar.ts ride4dof.ts lean.ts), `src/sim-core/content/cafe-racer.ts`,
`src/sim-core/content/moto-{classic,modern,telelever}.ts`, `tests/sim-core/moto*.test.ts`.
No edit to `simulation.ts` / `vehicle-models.ts` / `index.ts` is expected (the stub is already registered).

## Tasks
1. **Planar kit + content** — `planar.ts`: telescopic (prismatic along the steering axis), Telelever (A-arm + head
   ball joint, axis = head→joint), swingarm (direct shock), 4-bar linkage (circle–circle, branch continuity). LUT on a
   1 mm wheel-travel grid: points, shock length, MR, patch-point dx/dw (brake path), chain coupling, IC; static sag
   solved from the spring curve; summaries + KinematicsCurves. Content: vehicle + three presets (copy without digits).
2. **Ride + lean + model** — `ride4dof.ts` (heave, pitch, two hops; virtual-work coupling; stiction tanh; dual-rate /
   progressive springs; stops; g_eff; closed-form equilibrium reset, never throws), `lean.ts` (γ, crown correction,
   2nd-order tracker, inverse roll-balance steer, clamp, low-speed blend), `model.ts` (road inputs relative to the
   smooth road plane, vertical-curve acceleration, events, TRUE/POSE sample, restPose, setSuspension).
3. **Tests** — `tests/sim-core/moto.test.ts`: content consistency, pro-dive (AD<0) vs Telelever (AD>0) and
   fork compression vs pure load transfer at 0.6 g, rising-rate MR, trail formula, sag 25–40 %, bounce 2.0–2.8 Hz,
   braking pitch vs 08 §4.5 ±5 %, anti-squat = IC construction, lean closed form ±1° (flat + carousel), lean sign,
   countersteer, copy has no digits; `moto-sim.test.ts`: createSimulation runs a lap finite, seek at ≥200 lap
   times bounded and seek-vs-continuous agreement, setSuspension, golden hash, metrics report.
