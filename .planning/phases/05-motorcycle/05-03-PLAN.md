# 05-03 PLAN — Motorcycle integration

**Objective:** Car ↔ Motorcycle from the HUD and the phone remote; the cafe racer explorable like the car (three
presets one click apart, follow/explore/close-up with bike framings, 2-corner telemetry, bike insights, sane overlays,
an attract bike beat). Honest differences only; copy never ranks a type.

Corrections to ROADMAP text: right turn → bike leans right → roll > 0 (contract); Telelever trail falls far less
than the telescopics' (it does not grow); lean is not amplified.

## Tasks
1. **Hardpoint fixes (05-02 known issues).** Modern: rotate the monoshock (rockerShock + shockTop) about the rocker
   pivot by −30° — rigid rotation in the plane leaves the MR curve identical and moves the shock ≥ 36 mm clear of the
   tyre at every travel; visual brace moves ahead of the shock. Single-sided: shock y = the arm side, drop the visual
   workaround. Update hashes/tests that legitimately change.
2. **Registration + bike logic.** `motorcycle/index.ts` registers the vehicle, three presets and both visuals. Bike
   branches (by `vehicle.class` / corner count, not ids) in camera (chase height, side close-ups f = fork, r =
   swingarm/shock), attract (a bike beat), controls (class toggle already exists — verify), telemetry (2 corners),
   insights (fork dive + trail change under braking, lean, squat), overlays (2-wheel frame). Unit tests where logic is new.
3. **E2E** `tests/e2e/05-03-motorcycle.spec.ts`: toggle via HUD; presets one click; lean sign at the carousel
   (roll > 0); fork travel Classic > Telelever at the brake-zone `t`; close-ups; screenshots `05-03-*.png`.
4. (Second priority) engine looks: finned cylinders/head, shaped crankcase covers, fewer/thicker spokes.
