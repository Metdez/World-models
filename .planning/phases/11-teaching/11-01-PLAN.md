# 11-01 PLAN — Tyre VFX: friction rings and skid marks

Objective: the in-world grip lesson (ARCH-M2 §10.5; research m2/13 §devices 1, m2/19 §2.4, §6). Under each tyre a ring
whose radius is the model grip available there now (radius = 0.25 m · √(capacity / static capacity)), a dot for the force
used ((fx, fy) / capacity · radius, wheel frame); past the limit the ring turns warn, breaks into dashes and the dot opens
(colour + shape cue); skid marks only while past the limit, laid along the contact-patch path. Auto: rings only with the
engineer overlay (demand estimate), never marks. Labelled "model" (chip).

Owns: `src/features/tyre-vfx/**`, `tests/features/tyre-vfx.test.ts`, `tests/e2e/11-01-vfx.spec.ts`.

## Tasks
1. **Model + rings** (`model.ts`, `rings.ts`): pure per-corner mapping from CornerFrame (radius, dot, past-limit, excess).
   Past the limit: plant = normalised combined slip s > the tyre curve's peak slip (sim-core `tyreCoefficients`, the
   plant's own definition; gripUse cannot exceed 1 in the plant because |F| ≤ D); Auto = gripUse > 1 (demand estimate).
   Static capacity = peakMu(Fz_static)·Fz_static per axle from the vehicle. Bike / 2-corner frames → nothing. Rings: one
   instanced SDF quad (4 instances, 1 draw), oriented in the car's road plane by the wheel heading, px-constant AA
   strokes with a dark casing, distance fade, hidden in close-up.
2. **Marks + toggle + e2e** (`marks.ts`, `index.ts`, css): one shared ring buffer of quads (4096 segments, 1 draw),
   distance-throttled (0.15 m, folio-2025 `Tracks.js` MIT pattern re-expressed as CPU-written vertex attributes),
   intensity by how far past the limit, age fade near the overwrite point, distance fade, preallocated update ranges (no
   per-frame allocation); cleared on reset, cut, vehicle swap and seek. "Grip rings · model" chip in the action row
   (`sl-drive-action`), on by default outside Auto. Node tests + e2e screenshots (pad cornering, slide, split-μ lock-up).
