# 11-02 PLAN — Drive events, coach, audio

Objective: while the visitor drives, sim-core detectors notice the moments worth explaining (slide past the peak,
lift-off rotation, lock-up, wheelspin, an aid stepping in, a kerb or bump strike, a hard stop); right AFTER such a moment
a one-line coach explains it in plain words from templates filled with live values; insights stay with Auto; the
muted-by-default sound follows the real powertrain and warns before the grip limit. ARCH-M2 §7.11, §9, §10.5, §13;
research m2/13 (sparse, terminal-first, fading), m2/15 §3 (detected events only), m2/18 §2 (rpm/load, squeal onset 0.75,
surface noise); 09-04 suspension-type honesty report (no ranking, MacPherson vs double wishbone below notice).

Owned: `src/sim-core/drive/events.ts`, `src/features/{coach,insights,audio}/**`, `tests/sim-core/drive-events.test.ts`,
`tests/features/{coach,insights,audio}.test.ts`, `tests/e2e/11-02-coach.spec.ts`.

## Tasks
1. **Detectors** (`drive/events.ts`): `createDriveDetectors(vehicle?)` keeps the §7.1 shape
   `step(dt, view, input, out, moments?)`; slide per axle (mean |tan α| / tan α_peak(load) > 1.05 for 0.15 s, `lift`),
   lock-up (κ < −0.8, v > 3 m/s, brake > 0.3, 0.1 s), wheelspin (κ > 0.25, 0.2 s), aid rising edges; 0.5 s re-arm
   hysteresis and ≥ 0.3 s between events per kind/channel; extra moments outside the frozen DriveEvent union (kerb/bump
   strike, hard stop) in a second output. `frameDetectorInput(frame, input)` fills the input from a sampled frame.
   Zero-alloc between events, only `+−×÷`/sqrt/abs. The plant does not call the detectors yet (plant.ts is not mine):
   the hook is reported; the coach runs the same detectors on each rendered manual frame meanwhile (`shortcut:`).
   Tests on scripted plant runs + an autopilot lap (no false slide/lock/spin).
2. **Coach + insights gate** (`coach/rules.ts` pure + `index.ts` + `coach.css`; insights speak only in Auto): templates
   without digits, ≤ 1 line per 12 s, after-the-moment delay, max 3 per kind with growing spacing, one polite
   announcement per line, station card on zone entry (skipped right after a station reset), swap line without ranking.
3. **Audio**: Manual engine from `engineRpm` (idle..redline) and throttle; tyre cue from max grip use with onset 0.75;
   synthesised surface rumble from wheel travel rate with pavé/kerb texture; muted by default, ramps only (click-free).
   Unit tests bounded/finite; e2e on a private build: provoked slide + kerb strike → coach line, screenshots.
