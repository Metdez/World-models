## Tasks
1. **Detectors in the plant.** `DriveEvent` gains `strike {corner, kerb}` and `hard-stop {from}`. The plant creates
   `createDriveDetectors(vehicle)`, fills a preallocated `DetectorInput` from its own tyre outputs, ride state, command
   and aid flags once per fixed step, and pushes into `driveEvents` (moments copied over). Reset on engage, place and
   restore. Observational only: replay golden and Auto goldens unchanged. Coach: bus events only. Step time before/after.
2. **Manual HUD.** One slim bottom-centre row while driving: the pill holds speed/gear and icon buttons; secondary
   actions (Grip rings, Moment replay, Share, Stations, Assists, Compare) sit in a shared `.sl-drive-action` flex row
   that collapses into a compact icon group beside the pill; recording = a dot; the suspension selector compacts (no
   description line); the control hint fades. Bottom inset exposed only if a contract allows (else reported).
3. **Small screens.** Touch Manual at 844×390 and 1024×768: telemetry card compact/hidden while touch-driving, captions
   width-capped above the controls, no overlap with slider/pedals. `tests/e2e/11-05-layout.spec.ts` asserts overlaps,
   the bottom-centre height at 1280×720 / 1920×1080 and uncovered tyre contact points.
4. **Annex look + budget.** Edge lines, pad tonal variation/seams, concentric rings on the steering circle, gravel
   margin, low kerb, clearer braking lanes, a few instanced props (marker boards, light poles, a hut), softer horizon.
   Coarse ribbon LOD when the camera is in the annex (ride street at high ≤ 1.5 M triangles).
5. **Housekeeping.** `world-ground.test.ts` meaningful or gone; `world-zones` no-alloc test robust.
