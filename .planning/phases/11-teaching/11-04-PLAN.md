# 11-04 PLAN — Same line, different suspension (A/B ghost)

## Objective
MD-44: "Compare" (G or the chip in the drive action row) takes the last ≤ 30 s of the visitor's manual drive, has a
DISCLOSED driver model drive the visitor's line and speed with the same suspension (A/A control) and with another
(B), replays the visitor's run with B as a translucent ghost in lockstep, and shows distance-aligned traces (steer,
front/rear slip, sideslip, roll, tracking error) with one honest sentence. Differences are reported only above the
A/A noise floor by a clear margin; if the driver model cannot repeat the visitor's own run, the comparison says so and
draws no conclusion.

## Design (research m2/24 §d option B; ARCH-M2 §7.14, §13.3)
- Course (sim-core `ghost/ghost.ts`): headless exact re-simulation of the span (`runReplay`) in a fresh simulation;
  the comparison window = the longest stretch driven forward above walking pace with no reset/suspension/aids event;
  per 0.5 m of travel: x, y, u (→ `recordedLine`), the visitor's applied command (steer, pedals, handbrake), yaw
  rate, and the traces; the plant snapshot at the window start.
- Ghost driver (same for A and B; not the return autopilot): the visitor's own command at the same point of the line
  as feed-forward, plus line feedback (CG offset + course error, gains scaled by the speed-dependent yaw gain,
  bandwidth ≈ 2.5 rad/s), yaw-rate feedback against the visitor's yaw rate, a small offset integral, and speed PI on
  the visitor's speed. With the same suspension the feedback stays near zero (A/A ≈ the visitor); with another it
  supplies exactly the correction that suspension needs — that is the "steering effort" difference.
- Comparison (`ghost/compare.ts`): noise floor per channel = max |A' − visitor|; refuse when A' drifts > 0.25 m from
  the line or does not finish; B "lost" when > 0.75 m; a difference counts when its peak exceeds max(3 × floor,
  perceptual floor); sentence built from fixed templates (no digits) + sim values; never ranks types.
- Live ghost: a fresh simulation's plant with B, restored at the window start, stepped in lockstep with
  `DriveHost.replay.tick` while `drive:play` replays the span; sampled into its own TelemetryFrame each frame.
- Feature `ghost-lab`: chip in the action row (`.sl-drive-action`), target picker (other types + "same" control),
  ghost = a second vehicle visual from the registry with `setXray(1)` (its fresnel shell; opaque body meshes hidden,
  wheels share the shell material, amber tint) ≤ 40 draws; traces panel (2D canvas, HUD style) right column above
  the view switcher; disclosure caption always visible with the ghost/traces; clears on reset, vehicle/suspension
  swap, hand-back, another replay.
