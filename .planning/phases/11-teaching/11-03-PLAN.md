# 11-03 PLAN — Replays: recorder, format, share link, moment replay

## Objective
Deterministic input-log replays of Manual Drive runs (ARCH-M2 §7.10 F/G, §7.13; research m2/24 §b–c, m2/15 §1;
MD-43, MD-50): the live sim consumes the same quantised intents the recorder stores; a replay restores the recorded
start state and re-simulates bit-identically (same build), checked by CRC keyframes; moment replay (last ~4 s at 0.25×,
then manual at exactly the pre-replay state) and a `#r=` share link (≤ 60 s, deflate-raw + base64url) that plays on load
with a Replay chip and "Drive it yourself". Server save is out of scope (12-01/12-05).

## Tasks
1. **Format + recorder** (`src/sim-core/replay/{format,recorder}.ts`, `tests/sim-core/replay.test.ts`): `SLR1` binary
   (§7.13), `decodeReplayResult` with reasons (damaged / newer / mismatch), `paramsHash`, `stateChecksum`, `SIM_VERSION`;
   recorder with preallocated rings (intents 600 s, snapshot + CRC every 1 s), `window(s)`, `whole()`, `rewind`;
   zero allocation per step.
2. **Control/simulation replay mode** (`drive/control.ts`, `simulation.ts`): record manual runs (start snapshot = plant
   snapshot + HumanDriver state), suspension/aids/reset events, `play()` (fit check, header suspension, start restore,
   events at ticks, keyframe compare → resync from recorder snapshots or mark "may differ"), branching takeover, end →
   manual; `DriveHost.window/replay/stateChecksum`; `runReplay` headless helper (for 11-04).
   Golden `tests/sim-core/replay-golden.test.ts` (scripted 20 s; record → replay → identical CRC at every keyframe;
   SIM_VERSION pinned).
3. **Feature** (`src/features/replay/**`, `tests/features/replay.test.ts`, `tests/e2e/11-03-replay.spec.ts`): chip in
   `.sl-drive-action` (recording dot, Moment replay (Q), Share → link field + copy), `#r=` load → Replay state with
   progress + "Drive it yourself", graceful messages (too long, damaged/incompatible, other model, mismatch), size report.

## Known outside-file need
`src/sim-core/drive/plant.ts`: the public `restore()` zeroes the previous step's tyre outputs (fx, fy, slip ratio) that
the next step reads (compliance, aids, powertrain) — a mid-run restore diverges (probe: 0.37 m after 9 s). Minimal fix:
append those 12 values to the snapshot and re-apply them in the public restore only (no caller today; fault recovery
unchanged). Reported as a deviation.
