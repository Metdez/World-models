# 07-01 PLAN — Render performance + visual polish

**Objective:** make the 3D world read as premium on a big screen and keep the frame inside budget. Scope from the
orchestrator brief (supersedes the ROADMAP row's file list for this plan): `src/features/{renderer,environment,track,
car,vehicle-view,linkage-parts,camera,attract,teaching-overlays,motorcycle}/**`, `src/kit3d/**`,
`src/sim-core/moto/planar.ts` (one fix) and their tests. Contracts, worker, package.json frozen.

## Tasks
1. **Correctness fixes** — `clampIc`: an at-infinity IC is placed at IC_MAX so overlays fade it (no fake ring).
   Car close-up hides the opposite corner's construction. Attract section jumps timed in sim time (deterministic on
   any machine).
2. **Bike close-up + explore camera** — no forced x-ray for a 2-corner vehicle (the bike stays solid); clean side
   framings of fork and rear end, never seeing the underside on lean toward the lens; wheel/pinch zoom verified with
   sane min/max per vehicle; attract hero opens from a front three-quarter.
3. **Looks** — bike engine/crankcase, header routing, tank profile, brake lines (draw calls ≈ unchanged); car: strut
   top under the bonnet for all types, rear diffuser darker, paint hue, arch liner edge, x-ray shell readability.
4. **Performance** — per-frame allocations on the main thread in owned files, governor never oscillates (test), cheap
   AA on the low tier, draw calls / triangles per tier for car and bike recorded.
