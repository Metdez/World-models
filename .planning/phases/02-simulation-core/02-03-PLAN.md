# 02-03 PLAN — 7-DOF ride model and tyre grip indicator

**Objective:** the ride model of ARCHITECTURE §7.2.3: heave/roll/pitch of the sprung body plus four unsprung
hubs, driven by road heights under each wheel and the path accelerations ax/ay, with roll-centre (geometric) and
anti-pitch load transfer as link forces, elastic transfer through springs + ARB, asymmetric dampers, Coulomb
friction, progressive bump/droop stops, tyre lift-off clamp. 240 Hz semi-implicit Euler with implicit damping.
Plus the grip-use indicator (`gripUse`, a model figure, not a measurement).

**Owns:** `src/sim-core/ride/**` (`ride7dof.ts`, `tyre.ts`), `tests/sim-core/ride*.test.ts`,
`tests/fixtures/02-03-ride.ts` (synthetic GT-like parameters and kinematic curves; real curves come from 02-02).

**Depends on:** 01-02 math (`linsolve.solveInPlace`, `interp.interp1`), contracts (`units.G`,
`vehicle.TyreParams`, `suspension.KinematicsCurves`).

## Derivation (binding for the code; ARCHITECTURE §4 signs)
Coordinates q = (zs, φ, θ, zu1..4), deviations from static equilibrium; corner body height zb = zs + yφ − xθ;
travel (bump +) = zu − zb. Spring preload P_i = static sprung corner load (lever rule per axle), so travel 0 is
the static design ride height. Lateral (ay > 0 left turn): link lateral forces act at ground on the body (roll
torque ms·ay·h) plus a vertical link pair per axle ±ms_j·ay·h_rc/t (left hub up, right hub down, reaction on the
body) — exactly equivalent to the lateral force acting at the roll centre (fixes the double-count in research
05 §3.1, which adds the pair AND a torque about the roll axis). Unsprung inertia: direct tyre pair
Σm_u·h_u·ay/t. Tilt: +ms·g·(h − h_ra)·φ. Longitudinal: body torque −ms·ax·h; link pairs
front ±AD_f·ms·ax·h/L, rear ∓AD_r (anti-dive / anti-lift through links), unsprung pair Σm_u·h_u·ax/L.
ARB: F = k_arb·(travel_i − travel_other), k_arb = K_roll/t².

## Tasks
1. **Integrator** — `ride7dof.ts`: params → preloads, masses; force evaluation; step = (M + dt·C) v' = M v + dt·Q
   (C = dampers bump/rebound by travel-rate sign, tyre damping, linearised friction, dt·stop rate); `reset` =
   Newton solve of static equilibrium under the given input; helpers `wheelRateForRideFrequency`,
   `dampingForRatio`, `arbRateFromRollStiffness`, `rollCentreFromCurves`. Tests: static loads/deflections,
   lift-off clamp, asymmetric damping, determinism, zero allocation, step cost.
2. **Load transfer** — tests: steady-state cornering transfer per axle and in total vs closed form (incl. tyre
   compliance and tilt), roll gradient vs closed form, braking pitch with anti 0 / 0.25 vs closed form, signs.
3. **Grip + modal** — `tyre.ts`: `peakMu`, `gripUse`, `camberToRoad`; tests: monotone in |fy|, camber loss
   beyond 3°, load sensitivity. Modal tests: body ride frequency / damping ratio from free decay, hop period from
   impulse, energy non-growth, stiff-configuration stability and measured stiffness margin.
