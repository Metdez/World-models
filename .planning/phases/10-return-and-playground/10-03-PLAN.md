# 10-03 PLAN — Proving-ground visuals; terrain from GroundWorld

Objective: draw the annex (zones, wet patches, markings, cones, signs) on exactly the GroundWorld surface, and re-bake the
track terrain from `world.terrainHeight` (ARCH-M2 §8.4; 10-02 SUMMARY "What 10-03 needs").

Owns: `src/features/proving-ground/**`, `src/features/track/**`, `tests/features/{proving-ground,track}.test.ts`,
`tests/e2e/10-03-ground.spec.ts`.

## Tasks
1. **Zone meshes + materials** (`proving-ground/{surface,materials}.ts`): per zone rows (u) with their own columns (w)
   stitched by a merge walk (as the ribbon), extended by `ZONE_EDGE`; dense rows/columns on the ride-street features
   (bumps, sleepers, pothole 5 cm; washboard λ/12; pavé stone centres + joints); patch / lane edges as rows/columns so
   each quad has one surface; vertex height = `world.sample(x, y).h`; a triangle is kept only by the zone that owns its
   centroid (first footprint in priority order, else the nearest footprint within the band). Three chunks (north, pad,
   platform), groups asphalt / cobbles / wet asphalt / wet tiles (MeshPhysical clearcoat 1, roughness 0.15, ×0.65).
   Textures by the track's asset ids. Tier step low 2 / medium 1 / high 0.5 m, ≤ 60 k quads per zone.
2. **Props, markings, terrain switch, tests, e2e**: markings as one vertex-coloured decal mesh (lift 8 mm +
   polygonOffset; deviation from the shader approach: no per-pixel loop under SwiftShader); cones one InstancedMesh
   (lathe); station signs one merged canvas-atlas mesh; `track/terrain.ts` = adaptive quadtree (4 → 2 → 1 m where the
   linear error > 1 cm, crack-free fans) over `world.terrainHeight`; `track/ribbon.ts` cut under the spurs (columns at
   the footprint crossings, heights from `world.sample`, triangles inside a spur dropped); the local terrain maths
   removed (`groundHeight` re-exported from sim-core for the existing sim-core test). Node tests: vertex heights = world
   ±1 mm, interpolated surface vs world (zones, ride street features, terrain incl. the two-section gaps), ownership (no
   overlap), draw-call / triangle budgets per tier, no-world terrain = bare GroundWorld; e2e screenshots of five
   stations, overview, both spur joins, `?features=-proving-ground`.
