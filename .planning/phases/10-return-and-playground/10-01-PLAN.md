# 10-01 PLAN — Autopilot in the plant: Returning and latch

## Objective
Enter while driving hands the SAME dynamic car to an autopilot (PathDriver) wherever it is — on the grass, spun,
reversed, stopped, mid-slide — which brings it back to the driving line without hard braking and latches onto the Auto
lap without a visible jump (ARCH-M2 §7.9 PathDriver, §7.10 B/C/D, §11 T9/H2/H3). Never stuck: a 20 s (60 s off the
corridor) timeout releases with a cut.

## Owned files
`src/sim-core/drive/{reference,path-driver,control}.ts`, `src/sim-core/simulation.ts`,
`tests/sim-core/{path-driver,returning,continuity}.test.ts`.
Needed outside the list (no other executor owns them this wave; reported as a deviation): `drive/plant.ts`
(`releaseToPath` body, which throws today, + one `CarCore` method) and `car/car-model.ts` (that method: refit the Auto
reference plane `refZ/refSlope` so the Auto road inputs continue the plant's at the latch). Auto paths untouched.

## Tasks
1. **reference + PathDriver + recovery** (`reference.ts`, `path-driver.ts`, `path-driver.test.ts`): `loopLine` (global
   centreline projection → offset from the driving line incl. `lineOffset` knots, line heading/curvature as the speed
   profile defines them, profile speed/accel at s), `recordedLine` (open polyline for 11-04). PathDriver: Stanley +
   curvature feed-forward + yaw damping, profile speed tracking with accel feed-forward and PI, derate by error, the
   TRACK/RECOVER/BRAKE/TURNAROUND/RESUME machine with hysteresis, `converged` (held 1 s), bumpless engage (slews).
   Feedback braking capped (no hard brake outside BRAKE); the speed derate fades to exact when tracking well so the latch
   has no speed step. Lifted: Stanley / pure-pursuit structure from PythonRobotics (MIT, LICENSE re-checked).
   Test: T9 ISO 3888-1 on the loop DLC at the 80 km/h section speed inside the gates.
2. **control/simulation Returning + latch** (`control.ts`, `simulation.ts`, plant/car-model release): `request('auto')`
   from manual = Returning (`cut:false`), `immediate` = D (cut, rails at the projected lap time); latch on `converged`:
   lapTime = timeAtS(s), `releaseToPath(stateAt)`, events `converged` + `mode {cut:false}`; timeouts → cut; takeover
   from returning → manual. Tests: `returning.test.ts` (H2: 500 seeded starts ≤ 12 m, |ψ_e| ≤ 180°, v −5…40 m/s → latch
   ≤ 20 s in a 30 m corridor; 50 off-loop starts ≤ 60 s; no NaN; measured worst rejoin time, max decel, lat-g, steer
   rate; latch discontinuity of pose, speed, roll/pitch, travel, load), `continuity.test.ts` (H3 per-section roll /
   travel / speed vs Auto). Plant no-alloc/cost with the PathDriver.
