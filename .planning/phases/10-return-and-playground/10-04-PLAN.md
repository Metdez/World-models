# 10-04 PLAN — Drive UX v2: Returning UI, stations, gamepad, touch, rumble

Objective: the visitor hands the car back and the autopilot visibly returns it to the lap (or, from the annex, a short
fade); stations are a calm one-tap way to try an experiment; a standard gamepad and touch controls drive the same plant
as the keyboard (unchanged); subtle feature-detected rumble; out-of-bounds fades and resets. ARCH-M2 §9, §10.1-10.4;
research m2/07 §2-4, m2/18 §3, m2/22 §3. Orchestrator decisions 1-5 (timeout 40 s, annex release, stations as
experiments, devices + arbitration, OOB).

Owned: `src/features/drive/**`, `tests/features/drive*.test.ts`, `tests/sim-core/proving-ground-manoeuvres.test.ts`,
`tests/e2e/10-04-drive.spec.ts`; plus (orchestrator) `RETURN_TIMEOUT` in `src/sim-core/drive/control.ts` and its test
expectation (`tests/sim-core/returning.test.ts` strings), and the 08-03 e2e step that assumed Enter = immediate.

## Tasks
1. **Hand-back, stations, OOB** (`handback.ts` pure + `index.ts` + `drive.css`): Enter / pill / idle → on the loop
   (zone `loop`, |lineOffset| ≤ 40 m) `request('auto')` = Returning with a "Returning… · Rejoin now" chip in the pill
   (state from `DriveHost.autopilot`); from the annex a brief "Returning to the lap" + fade + immediate release. Rejoin =
   immediate (fade). Fade overlay 0.35 s on every cut release (`mode{auto,cut:true}`), none on the latch; reduced motion
   = cut. Stations button + popover (name, what to try, what to watch, tyre-grip note), in Auto titled "Drive the proving
   ground" (takes the wheel and spawns). OOB event → fade + reset to the nearest valid point (loop line or nearest
   station spawn). Timeout constant 20 → 40 s.
2. **Gamepad + touch + rumble** (`gamepad.ts`, `touch.ts`, `rumble.ts`): standard mapping only, axial dead zone 0.12 +
   exponent 1.6 on left-stick X, trigger dead zone 0.04, RT/LT analog, A toggle, X handbrake, Y reset (edges), re-read
   every frame, hot-plug note + announcement; rumble dual-rumble when `effects` has it, weak = roughness (RMS travel
   rate) + limit, strong = bump-stop/kerb/ABS pulses, 120 ms at ≤ 20 Hz, cap 0.5, off outside Manual, toggle in the
   Assists popover (shown only with a rumble-capable pad). Touch (coarse pointer, Manual): left steering slider (relative
   drag, spring back, pointer capture, `touch-action: none`), right GAS above BRAKE (≥ 48 px); only the controls take
   pointer events (orbit stays on the rest of the canvas). Arbiter unchanged (most recent wins, 1 s hysteresis).
3. **T10 + tests + e2e**: unit tests for every pure piece (`tests/features/drive-input.test.ts`); T10 ISO 3888-2 on
   the pad per suspension with the PathDriver on a recordedLine through the gate centres, max pass speed table printed;
   e2e `10-04-drive.spec.ts`: Returning → AUTO latch via `advance`, Rejoin, spawn + stations popover, annex hand-back,
   gamepad (mocked `navigator.getGamepads`) steer sign, touch drive (844×390, 1024×768), reduced-motion fade = cut;
   screenshots.
